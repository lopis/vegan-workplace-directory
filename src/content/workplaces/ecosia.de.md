As a company in the climate sector, Ecosia is generally very vegan friendly, but not exclusively vegan or vegetarian.

The office in Berlin is stocked with vegan snacks likes fresh and dried fruits, muesli, nuts, cookies, chocolates. Some snacks are sometimes vegetarian, like milk chocolate. The office only offers oat milk - no animal milk.

Company lunch is provided on Wednesday that is vegetarian with vegan option. Vegan breakfast is provided on Fridays.

Company events are usually vegetarian with vegan options. This includes pizza nights, company parties, and other company outings.

Company-sponsored team events are not necessarily vegan nor vegetarian - by policy or by default - as most employees are not vegan themselves. So team dinners require explicit requests to be vegan friendly. That is rarely an issue since team dinners typically happen in Berlin.
